# The Relative

## Bio

Someone you love became obsessed with Mourning Bluff Manor and disappeared while trying to reach it. The authorities found no evidence that they ever entered the estate. When you learned that the new inheritor was allowing a private investigation, you demanded a place in the group. You are not interested in ghost stories. You want to discover what happened and, against all reason, still hope to bring them home.

## Stats

**Nerve +4 | Notice +2 | Rapport +3 | Reason +0 | Vigor +1**

## Weakness — Survivor's Guilt

I already lost someone to this house, and I cannot bear to let it happen again.

If a member of the group goes missing, discard your visible Premonition without revealing the next card. A character is missing when their location is unknown or the house has cut them off from the group, not merely because they willingly entered a nearby room.

Until you are reunited with the missing character, make every test using the top card of your deck without looking at it. After each test, discard the card used but do not reveal the next one. If several characters are missing, your Premonition remains hidden until everyone who is still missing has returned.

When you are reunited, turn the top card of your deck faceup. It becomes your visible Premonition normally.

## Gift — Take My Future

*If someone has to face what is coming, let it be me.*

Once per room, when another character is about to make a test, you may exchange your visible Premonition with theirs. They make the test using your former card; their former card becomes your Premonition.

You cannot use this Gift while Survivor's Guilt has left your Premonition hidden.

## Tool — Family Photograph

*You carry a slightly faded Polaroid of the missing person laughing in a sunlit park. They are caught mid-laugh with their head turned slightly away from the camera, one hand raised as if they had just tried to hide their face. Green grass, a picnic table, and several distant trees fill the background. It was taken before they became obsessed with Mourning Bluff Manor.*

The photograph may change in response to the group's actions. When you examine it, ask the GM what it currently shows. The GM describes one subtle difference or tells you that it remains unchanged. Changes might affect the missing person's expression, surroundings, clothing, position, or something previously unnoticed in the background.

## Secret

Create a Secret that your character must hide from the rest of the group. It should be something that could cause conflict if the others discovered it.
