# The Priest

## Bio

You have spent years listening to people describe grief, guilt, visions, and experiences they believe were supernatural. Most needed compassion more than an exorcism. When the new inheritor sought help concerning Mourning Bluff Manor, you agreed to enter, investigate its stories, and determine whether the house requires a blessing, a more serious spiritual intervention, or no religious response at all. You are here to protect the living before passing judgment on the dead.

## Stats

**Nerve +0 | Notice +2 | Rapport +4 | Reason +3 | Vigor +1**

## Weakness — Beyond Doctrine

I know God's creation has an order; whatever is in this house must obey it.

When you confront a supernatural phenomenon with prayer, scripture, the crucifix, or a religious rite, the GM tells you whether it recognizes what you are doing.

If it does not recognize or respond to the act, take -3 on your next test involving that phenomenon.

The penalty ends after that test, regardless of its result.

## Gift — Sacrifice the Crucifix

*Faith is not the object in my hand; it is what I am willing to surrender.*

Once per game, when any character reveals a Major Arcana, you may expend your crucifix to prevent that Major from activating.

Remove the Major from the deck and set it aside for the remainder of the game. The crucifix is destroyed, broken, or rendered spiritually powerless and can no longer be used as a Tool.

## Tool — Crucifix

*You carry a small silver crucifix on a black cord, its edges worn smooth from years of being held during prayer.*

It has accompanied you throughout your ministry. It may be held, displayed, placed upon a threshold, incorporated into a rite, offered to someone in need, or used in any other way the object and your faith reasonably permit.

Spirits may recognize, respect, fear, resent, or ignore it according to what they are and what the symbol means to them. The crucifix offers no automatic protection merely because it is present.

## Secret

Create a Secret that your character must hide from the rest of the group. It should be something that could cause conflict if the others discovered it.
