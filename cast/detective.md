# The Detective

## Bio

You spent years investigating disappearances connected to Mourning Bluff Manor. What began as an overlooked pattern became the only case that mattered to you. Your superiors called your conclusions speculative, then reckless, and finally removed you from the investigation. You are officially on administrative leave and have been ordered to stay away. You have entered the manor anyway, determined to find the evidence that proves the disappearances are connected—and that you were right to keep digging.

## What Is Established

You found a credible pattern linking multiple disappearances to the manor, but you could not prove the connection through official channels. Your investigation cost you professionally, and entering the property violates direct instructions. You still believe the missing deserve an answer. Your name, age, gender, appearance, occupation, relationships, and personal history are yours unless established above.

## Make the Character Your Own

Choose the agency, jurisdiction, and kind of investigative work you did. Decide whether you were once careful and respected, always difficult, or made one catastrophic judgment call. Your investment may be professional, personal, moral, or all three. Define one victim whose case you cannot set aside and the investigative habit your colleagues found impossible to tolerate.

## Decide Before Play

- Which disappearance transformed the manor from a theory into an obsession?

- What evidence did your superiors dismiss?

- Who in the group reminds you of someone you failed to protect?

## Stats

**Nerve +1 | Notice +4 | Rapport +0 | Reason +3 | Vigor +2**

## Weakness — Tunnel Vision

They removed me from the case because I could not let it go, and I still cannot leave a question unanswered.

When you fail a Notice test, you refuse to willingly leave the room until you succeed at another test of any kind.

If you are forced to leave before succeeding, discard your visible Premonition without revealing the next card. Continue making tests blindly until you succeed at a test. After that success resolves, reveal a new Premonition normally.

A success with a consequence counts as succeeding.

## Gift — Follow a Lead

*There is always another lead; I only have to choose the right one.*

Once per room, when you would reveal a new Premonition, reveal the top two cards of your deck instead.

Choose one to become your new Premonition and place the other on the bottom of your deck.

Only the chosen card becomes active. If it is a Major Arcana, resolve it immediately. A Major placed on the bottom does not activate.

## Tool — Flashlight

*You carry a heavy black metal flashlight with a wide glass lens, a ridged grip, and a sliding switch worn smooth by years of use. Its casing is scratched and dented, but its strong white beam still cuts cleanly through the dark.*

While it is working and pointed toward something, its beam illuminates ordinary darkness. You may carry it, hand it to someone, set it down, signal with it, or use it as the durable handheld object that it is.

Its light does not automatically reveal anything concealed by walls, fog, supernatural darkness, or an effect that prevents something from being perceived.

## Secret

Create a Secret that your character must hide from the rest of the group. It should be something that could cause conflict if the others discovered it.
