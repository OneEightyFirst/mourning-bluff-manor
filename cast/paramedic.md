# The Paramedic

## Bio

One of the people entering Mourning Bluff Manor is your friend, and you insisted on coming because someone needed to take the ordinary dangers seriously. You do not necessarily believe the house is haunted, but you do believe in rotten floorboards, rusty nails, asbestos, unstable stairs, and being hours away from medical help. Once people start getting hurt, it does not matter who brought you here. You are the person with the training to keep them alive.

## What Is Established

Choose one of the other selected characters as your friend. You came partly because of them and will not casually abandon them. You are trained to provide emergency medical aid, but the manor can create injuries and conditions that do not behave like ordinary harm. Your name, age, gender, appearance, occupation, relationships, and personal history are yours unless established above.

## Make the Character Your Own

Your concern for your friend does not have to be your only reason for coming. You might follow true-crime cases, explore abandoned places, collect local folklore, have family ties to the area, or simply distrust the plan enough to supervise it yourself. Decide where you work, what kind of emergencies shaped you, and whether your competence makes you calm, controlling, compassionate, or all three.

## Decide Before Play

- Which character is your friend, and what have the two of you already survived together?

- What personal interest in the manor are you reluctant to admit?

- What kind of emergency still makes it difficult for you to stay objective?

## Stats

**Nerve +3 | Notice +1 | Rapport +0 | Reason +2 | Vigor +4**

## Weakness — Do No Harm

When someone is hurt, I have one job. If I fail at it, I start expecting the worst.

When you fail a test made to provide medical aid to someone who has suffered physical harm, look at the top three cards of your deck instead of revealing your next Premonition.

Place the lowest-valued card on top of your deck faceup as your new Premonition. Put the other two on the bottom of your deck in any order. If cards are tied for the lowest value, choose between them. For this Weakness, a Major Arcana is lower than any Minor Arcana; if it becomes your Premonition, it activates normally.

## Gift — In Reserve

*I prepare because preparation is what keeps an emergency from becoming a tragedy.*

Once per room, you may set your visible Premonition aside faceup without playing or discarding it, then reveal your next Premonition normally.

You may use the reserved card for a later test instead of your current Premonition. Afterward, discard the reserved card; your current Premonition remains in place.

You can hold only one card in reserve. A Major cannot be placed in reserve because it activates as soon as it reaches the top of your deck.

## Tool — Medical Bag

*You carry a compact red trauma bag made from heavy waterproof fabric, with reflective strips, reinforced handles, and several zippered compartments. Inside are gloves, gauze, bandages, medical tape, antiseptic, trauma shears, a tourniquet, instant cold packs, a folding splint, a stethoscope, a penlight, and a small supply of common emergency medications.*

You can use these supplies to stop bleeding, clean and dress wounds, support an injured limb, manage shock, administer appropriate medication, or provide other medical aid. The bag and everything inside it may also be used in any other way the physical objects reasonably permit.

The bag cannot restore Grounding or cure possession, supernatural transformation, or other conditions that are not ordinary physical harm.

## Secret

Create a Secret that your character must hide from the rest of the group. It should be something that could cause conflict if the others discovered it.
