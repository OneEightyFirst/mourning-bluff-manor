# The Inheritor

## Bio

You are an ordinary person who has unexpectedly become the legal owner of Mourning Bluff Manor. You have always been more comfortable with training, competition, and physical problems than old documents or ghost stories, and you want nothing to do with the house. Your mother was obsessed with it, but she died when you were very young. After her death, your father destroyed everything she had collected about the manor and refused to speak of it again. Now the estate has passed to you, bringing her obsession back into your life. You have come to understand why she cared about this place and decide what must be done with it.

## What Is Established

The manor has legally passed to you. Your mother investigated it before her death, and your father deliberately erased what he could of that connection. You invited or permitted the others to enter because you need answers before you sell, preserve, destroy, or abandon the estate. Your name, age, gender, appearance, occupation, relationships, and personal history are yours unless established above.

## Make the Character Your Own

Choose the life you built far away from the manor. You might be an athlete, coach, tradesperson, outdoor guide, martial artist, or simply someone who trusts practical action more than old stories. Decide what you remember about your mother, whether you resent or understand your father, and what you hoped the sale of the property would make possible. You may care deeply about the family history, want nothing to do with it, or feel both at once.

## Decide Before Play

- What ordinary life did you expect to be living before the manor became yours?

- What is the one memory of your mother that your father could not destroy?

- Which person in the group did you personally ask to come, and why do you trust them?

## Stats

**Nerve +3 | Notice +1 | Rapport +2 | Reason +0 | Vigor +4**

## Weakness: Denial

I cannot believe the supernatural is real; it cannot be real, or I will never sell this godforsaken house.

When you willingly participate in an action whose stated purpose is to communicate with, obey, appease, summon, or banish a supernatural entity, discard your visible Premonition without revealing the next card.

Make your next test blindly. After it resolves, reveal a new Premonition normally.

## Gift: Clean Slate

*No, I refuse to accept that this is what happens next.*

Once per room, before making a test, turn your visible Premonition facedown and shuffle it into your remaining deck. Reveal a new Premonition normally.

Do not shuffle your discard pile. If the newly revealed card is a Major Arcana, it activates normally.

## Tool: Skeleton Key

*You carry a long iron skeleton key with an oval bow, a narrow shaft, and uneven teeth polished bright from use. A faded paper tag tied to it reads 669 GALLOWS WAY, MOURNING BLUFF, MAINE.*

The key was included with the estate papers. It fits many of the manor's original interior locks and may be used in any other way a small metal key reasonably permits.

The key does not automatically open modern locks, combination locks, blocked doors, or locks the house has created or altered.

## Secret

Create a Secret that your character must hide from the rest of the group. It should be something that could cause conflict if the others discovered it.
