# The Psychiatrist

## Bio

You were the attending psychiatrist responsible for the last known person to enter Mourning Bluff Manor. They returned alive but profoundly disturbed, unable to separate memories from hallucinations or describe the house without contradicting themselves. Nothing you tried restored them, and their condition has continued to deteriorate. You have entered the manor to understand what happened to your patient and determine whether the source was trauma, delusion, or something you were never trained to diagnose.

## Stats

**Nerve +1 | Notice +0 | Rapport +4 | Reason +3 | Vigor +2**

## Weakness — Fear of Isolation

I could not bring my patient back from what they experienced, and I cannot face this house without another living person nearby.

Whenever you fail a test while alone, lose 1 Grounding.

You are alone when no other player character is present in the same room. Spirits, apparitions, and other inhabitants of the house do not count.

## Gift: Reframe

*I cannot change an experience, but I can change the meaning it carries.*

Once per room, before making a test, you may change the suit of your visible Minor Arcana.

Its value remains unchanged. Treat it as the chosen suit for that test and for any effect that refers to its suit. After the test, it returns to its printed suit before being discarded.

## Tool: Microcassette Recorder

*You carry a palm-sized microcassette recorder with a scratched plastic casing, a built-in speaker, and large mechanical buttons that click loudly when pressed. The cassette inside contains recordings of your final sessions with the patient, and the carrying case holds several blank tapes and spare batteries.*

The recordings preserve the patient's actual voice as they describe memories, dreams, fears, and contradictory experiences inside Mourning Bluff Manor. You may rewind, replay, pause, compare sessions with the house, let others listen, or refer back to anything the patient said.

The sessions are fixed, but the patient may have been mistaken, confused, deceptive, or accurate in ways that were impossible to recognize outside the manor. The recorder can also make new recordings and may be used in any other way the physical device reasonably permits.

## Secret

Create a Secret that your character must hide from the rest of the group. It should be something that could cause conflict if the others discovered it.
