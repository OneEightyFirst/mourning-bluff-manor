# Choose Your Character

Five people will enter Mourning Bluff Manor. Each has a reason to join the investigation, and any one of them may have gathered the others on behalf of the manor's new inheritor.

## The Historical Archaeologist

Someone anonymously sent you a box containing photographs, floor plans, correspondence, and property records from Mourning Bluff Manor that have never appeared in any archive. The documents establish that a previously unknown inheritor now owns the estate. The inheritor has asked you to investigate the property and prove that its infamous hauntings are tricks, misunderstandings, or stories grown wild with age.

**Stats:** Nerve +2 | Notice +4 | Rapport +1 | Reason +3 | Vigor +0

---

## The Relative

Someone you love became obsessed with Mourning Bluff Manor and disappeared while trying to reach it. The authorities found no evidence that they ever entered the estate. When you learned that the new inheritor was allowing a private investigation, you demanded a place in the group. You are not interested in ghost stories. You want to find out what happened and, against all reason, still hope to bring them home.

**Stats:** Nerve +4 | Notice +2 | Rapport +3 | Reason +0 | Vigor +1

---

## The Fraudulent Medium

You make a living pretending to speak with the dead. Outwardly, you perform with absolute confidence; privately, you believe you are a fraud who has mistaken instinct and coincidence for something more. But once—and only once—you had a vision you could not explain. You saw Mourning Bluff Manor, including details you later confirmed had never been made public. You have come to the house grasping for proof that the vision was real and that perhaps you have not been pretending all this time.

**Stats:** Nerve +2 | Notice +3 | Rapport +4 | Reason +1 | Vigor +0

---

## The Paramedic

One of the people entering Mourning Bluff Manor is your friend, and you insisted on coming because someone needed to take the ordinary dangers seriously. You do not necessarily believe the house is haunted, but you do believe in rotten floorboards, rusty nails, asbestos, unstable stairs, and being hours away from medical help. Once people start getting hurt, it does not matter who brought you here. You are the person with the training to keep them alive.

**Stats:** Nerve +3 | Notice +1 | Rapport +0 | Reason +2 | Vigor +4

---

## The Psychic Researcher

You study clairvoyance, precognition, and anomalous perception through controlled experiments rather than seances or superstition. An anonymous source sent you decades of test results associated with Mourning Bluff Manor. The data contains patterns too consistent to dismiss and predictions recorded before the events they describe. Access to the house offers the chance to reproduce the results under controlled conditions—or finally identify the flaw that has fooled you.

**Stats:** Nerve +3 | Notice +2 | Rapport +1 | Reason +4 | Vigor +0

---

## The Psychiatrist

You were the attending psychiatrist responsible for the last known person to enter Mourning Bluff Manor. They returned alive but profoundly disturbed, unable to separate memories from hallucinations or describe the house without contradicting themselves. Nothing you tried restored them, and their condition has only deteriorated. You have entered the manor to understand what happened to your patient and determine whether the source of their illness was trauma, delusion, or something you were never trained to diagnose.

**Stats:** Nerve +1 | Notice +0 | Rapport +4 | Reason +3 | Vigor +2

---

## The Detective

You spent years investigating disappearances connected to Mourning Bluff Manor. What began as an overlooked pattern became the only case that mattered to you. Your superiors called your conclusions speculative, then reckless, and finally removed you from the investigation. You are officially on administrative leave and have been ordered to stay away. You have entered the manor anyway, determined to find the evidence that will prove the disappearances are connected and that you were right to keep digging.

**Stats:** Nerve +1 | Notice +4 | Rapport +0 | Reason +3 | Vigor +2

---

## The Priest

You have spent years listening to people describe grief, guilt, visions, and experiences they believe were supernatural. Most needed compassion more than an exorcism. When the new inheritor sought help concerning Mourning Bluff Manor, you agreed to enter, investigate the stories, and determine whether the house requires a blessing, a more serious spiritual intervention, or no religious response at all. You are here to protect the living before passing judgment on the dead.

**Stats:** Nerve +0 | Notice +2 | Rapport +4 | Reason +3 | Vigor +1

---

## The Dreamer

You have dreamed of Mourning Bluff Manor for months. Night after night you walk through its rooms, recognize people you have never met, and wake before reaching the same locked door. Recently the dreams changed: a group of visitors entered the house together, and one of them was you. When you discovered that an investigation was actually being organized, you contacted the others because you already knew where and when they would meet.

**Stats:** Nerve +3 | Notice +4 | Rapport +2 | Reason +1 | Vigor +0

---

## The Inheritor

You are an ordinary person who has unexpectedly become the legal owner of Mourning Bluff Manor. You have always been more comfortable with training, competition, and physical problems than old documents or ghost stories, and you want nothing to do with the house. Your mother was obsessed with it, but she died when you were very young. After her death, your father destroyed everything she had collected about the manor and refused to speak of it again. Now the estate has passed to you, bringing her obsession back into your life. You have come to understand why she cared about this place and decide what must be done with it.

**Stats:** Nerve +3 | Notice +1 | Rapport +2 | Reason +0 | Vigor +4
