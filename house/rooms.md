## Newly approved room awaiting placement

### The Tower

A circular, windowless chamber formed by the surviving buried foundation of the Tower. Its floor is packed earth. The nearly black masonry contains sealed openings suggesting stairs or passages that no longer exist. Heavy unlit candles remain in iron wall sconces installed by the Fellows, alongside corroded experimental fittings. The chamber's interior is wider than the Tower's exterior circumference should have allowed, and its dimensions and angles do not behave consistently.

The complete draft description currently remains in the design conversation and should be assigned a room number only after the basement sequence is mapped.
